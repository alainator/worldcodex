# Writing Standards

## Science Files

**Voice and tense.** Academic third-person throughout. Present tense for laws
and definitions. Past tense for observations and experimental results.

**Prose structure.** Full connected paragraphs, not bullet lists. Every
subsection explains WHY, not just WHAT. Transition sentences connect
subsections into a continuous argument.

**No meta-commentary.** Never reference a file's own structure. Forbidden:
"This section establishes ...", "The following addresses ...", "This resolves ..."

**No narrative content.** No character names, civilization names, or story
references in science directories. Application examples belong in applied
directories.

**In-universe terminology in science files.** Use the universe's technical
terms, not human-readable translations.

**Citations.** Inline format with ## References section. Each entry includes
a one-sentence annotation.

## Worldbuilding Files

Voice varies by content type. Human-readable names appropriate here.

## Anti-Patterns (Avoid in All Files)

- Replace polished generality with concrete anchors
- Guard against fake specificity
- Break templated patterns
- Self-audit: draft → audit → rewrite
